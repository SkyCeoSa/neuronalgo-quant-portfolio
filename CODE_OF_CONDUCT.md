# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This repository welcomes technical review and collaboration across experience levels and backgrounds. Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented. Critique code, methodology, and claims rather than people.

Examples of constructive participation include:

- explaining reproducibility concerns with concrete steps or references;
- distinguishing observations from assumptions and opinions;
- acknowledging uncertainty in financial or statistical results;
- respecting licensing, attribution, and privacy boundaries.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or demeaning language, threats, doxxing, publishing another person's private account or financial information, deliberate disclosure of credentials, spam, or repeated bad-faith disruption.

## Financial and research discussions

This is a research/software repository, not a venue for soliciting managed accounts or guaranteeing investment outcomes. Do not present backtests, simulations, or demo metrics as certain future returns. Discussions about performance should identify the source, period, methodology, and relevant limitations.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary repository conduct issues, use GitHub's reporting/moderation features or contact a repository maintainer through the issue/PR system. Do not place sensitive personal or security details in a public issue.

If private contact is necessary, use the [founder LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/massah). Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may restrict participation when behavior violates this code.

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces administered by the maintainers.
